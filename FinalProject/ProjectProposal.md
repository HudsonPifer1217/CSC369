## 1.
The question I want to answer is: What factors most strongly predict flight delays in U.S. domestic air travel, and how do these factors vary by airport and airline?

## 2.
This is my own question. I think it is worth asking because it can help me, a consumer, have a better understanding of what causes delays when I am traveling by airfair. It would also give me insight into what airlines experience more delays. 

## 3.
My hypothesis is that weather and airport congestion account for a larger share of severe delays than airline-specific factors, but the relative importance varies by airport and season.
